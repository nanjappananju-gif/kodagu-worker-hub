Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This privacy policy explains how Kodagu Worker Hub ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service to connect with local service workers or customers in Kodagu district, Karnataka.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and phone number, provided when you register.
  • Location details: your taluk and village/hobli, used to match you with nearby customers or workers. We do not collect continuous GPS tracking.
  • Worker profile details: for service providers — bio, years of experience, skills/categories, and verification status.
  • Booking & activity data: booking requests, job status, reviews and ratings you give or receive, and in-app notifications.
  • Subscription data: for workers, your subscription plan and status, used for billing and access purposes.

How we use your information

  • To match customers with nearby, relevant service workers.
  • To let a worker and customer contact each other directly once a booking is accepted.
  • To verify worker profiles before they can accept jobs.
  • To show ratings, reviews, and job history within the app.
  • To send in-app and notification updates about your bookings.

Sharing your information

Your phone number is only shared with the other party in a booking, and only after a worker accepts the job — never before, and never publicly. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure provider to store and secure app data.

Your choices

You can update your name, phone number, location, and (for workers) your skills and bio at any time from within the app's Edit Profile screen. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

Data retention

We retain your account and booking data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Children's privacy

Kodagu Worker Hub is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
